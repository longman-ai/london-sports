## 1. Profile Setup

### Username Suggestions
| Priority | Username | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|

### Link in Bio Strategy

**Go direct to the website** — not Linktree. Here's why:
- You're driving traffic to one destination (the directory)
- Fewer clicks = higher conversion
- Builds domain authority via social referral traffic
- Cleaner, more professional

**Link:** `londonsportscommunity.co.uk`

**Later (when you have 3+ destinations):** Switch to Linktree or Stan Store with:
- Browse the directory
- Read the blog
- Submit your group
- Latest borough spotlight

### Profile Picture

Use the **London Sports Community logo** in emerald green on a **white background**. Ensure:
- Logo is centred with padding (doesn't touch edges)
- Readable at small sizes (mobile thumbnail is ~36px)
- If the full logo is hard to read small, use just the icon/monogram
- Export at 320×320px minimum

## 6. Engagement Strategy

### Week 1 Follow Strategy

**Follow 50-100 accounts across these categories:**

| Category | Examples to find | How many |
|----------|-----------------|----------|
| London sports clubs/groups | Search sport + London | 20-25 |
| London fitness pages | Gyms, PT accounts, fitness influencers | 10-15 |
| Borough community pages | @hackneygazette, local pages | 10-15 |
| London lifestyle/events | @timeoutlondon style accounts | 5-10 |
| Sports brands with London presence | Local branches, ambassadors | 5-10 |

**Don't follow/unfollow spam.** Follow accounts you'd genuinely want to engage with. Quality > quantity.

### Comment Strategy

**Daily target:** 10-15 genuine comments on relevant London sports content.

**Comment formula:**
- Be specific to their content (not generic "great post! 🔥")
- Add value or ask a question
- Don't plug your account in comments (ever)

**Good examples:**
- "That pitch at [park] is class, we've got a few groups listed there too"
- "Padel is taking over London honestly, where's this court?"
- "This looks like such a good session, whereabouts in London?"

**Bad examples:**
- "Check out our page! 🔥🔥🔥"
- "Nice! 💪"
- "Follow us for more London sports content"

### Story Engagement (Daily)

Run **2-3 interactive Stories per week:**

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Poll** | "Football or running?" / "Morning or evening sessions?" |
| **Question box** | "What sport do you wish you could try?" |
| **Quiz** | "How many boroughs in London?" / "Which borough has the most parks?" |
| **This or That** | "5-a-side or 11-a-side?" / "Gym or team sport?" |
| **Countdown** | For blog drops or new borough spotlights |

### Repost/Share Strategy

**When group owners or users tag you:**
1. Repost to Stories immediately (within a few hours)
2. Add a "🔥 Love seeing this!" or relevant comment sticker
3. DM them a thank you: "Thanks for the shoutout! Let us know if your listing needs updating 💬"
4. If it's great content, ask permission to repost to feed

**Proactive outreach to listed groups:**
- DM groups after listing them: "Hey! We've just listed [Group Name] on London Sports Community — a free directory helping people find groups like yours. Here's your listing: [link]. Feel free to share it! 🙌"
- Don't ask for anything in return. Just give value.
- If they share it, you get free reach.

### DM Response Templates

**When someone asks about groups:**
```
Hey! What sport and area are you looking for? I can point you to some groups 🏃‍♂️
```

**When a group wants to be listed:**
```
That's great! You can submit your group here: [link to submit page] — it's completely free. We'll get you listed asap 🙌
```

**When someone says thanks:**
```
Love to hear it! Let us know how your first session goes 💪
```
