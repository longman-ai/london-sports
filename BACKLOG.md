Reach out to every group/venue we've listed to verify their info and build relationships.

### Email/DM Template:
```
Hi [Name],

I'm building London Sports Community (londonsports.vercel.app) — a free directory 
helping Londoners discover local sports groups by borough.

Your group [Group Name] is already listed and we're driving traffic to your page.

[Link to listing on browse page]

If anything needs updating, or you'd like a "verified" badge on your listing,
```

### Outreach Channels (in order of priority):
1. **Instagram DM** — most groups have active IG, casual and quick
2. **Email** — for venues/clubs with contact emails on their website
3. **Meetup message** — for groups found via Meetup
